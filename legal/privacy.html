Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Zorlii handles when you use the Zorlii website or mobile app.

HomeTerms of UseAccount deletion

Information you provide

  • Account details such as your email address, username, display name, and password. Passwords are stored as password hashes, not as readable passwords.
  • Your profile photo, biography, interests, and any posts, photos, videos, replies, or direct messages you choose to share.
  • Follows, reactions, bookmarks, account preferences, and reports or other information you send to support or moderation.
  • Confirmation that you are at least 18 and accept the Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy. Zorlii does not ask you to enter your birth date for this confirmation.

Information used to operate the service

Zorlii processes session and security information to sign you in, protect your account, deliver email verification or password-reset codes, and provide requested app features. Email delivery and infrastructure providers may process the information needed to perform those services.

If you enable phone notifications, Zorlii stores your device push token and sends activity alerts through Expo's push service and the relevant Apple or Google push service. Alerts use limited information such as the sender's username and the type of activity; message contents are not included. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.

Profile recommendations are optional and compare opted-in profile interests with real public posts. Local context help is also optional: text you submit is processed to select relevant sentences for that request and is not saved as a conversation or used to generate an answer.

How information is shared

Your public posts and profile details are visible according to the audience controls you choose. Followers-only and private posts are served according to their selected visibility. Direct messages are available to participants in that conversation. Reports are available to authorized moderators for review.

Zorlii does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that host the app, store its data, or deliver account emails, and may be disclosed when required by law or to protect users and the service.

Retention and your choices

You can edit your profile, export a copy of account data, block users, or permanently delete your account in the app. Account deletion removes your profile and account-owned posts, replies, follows, reactions, bookmarks, notifications, and messages you sent. Messages sent by other participants remain in their accounts. See Account deletion for instructions.

Some information may remain in backups for a limited period or where retention is required to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or protect the service.

Security and age

Zorlii uses reasonable safeguards, including hashed passwords and protected sessions, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Zorlii is intended only for people aged 18 or older. If you believe a person under 18 has created an account, contact us.
