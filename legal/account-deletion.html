Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to your Zorlii account.
  2. Open your profile and choose Delete account.
  3. Enter your current password and username to confirm, then confirm permanent deletion.

Account deletion removes your account, profile, posts and their media, replies, follows, reactions, bookmarks, notifications, and messages you sent. Messages sent by other participants remain available in their accounts. Deletion is permanent.

If you cannot use the app

Email benefit19990@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Include your Zorlii username and state that you want your account deleted. Do not include your password or a verification code. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify that you own the account before processing the request.
